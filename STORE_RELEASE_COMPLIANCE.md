# Store Release Compliance

Last updated: 2026-10-07

Production submission is blocked until every applicable item is verified against the exact release candidate: successful build/test/lint and unique version; current platform SDK/API requirements; least-privilege permissions; accurate public privacy policy and Google Play Data safety / Apple App Privacy / Microsoft declarations; account/data deletion where applicable; secure transport, secrets, backups and logs; truthful feature/safety claims; clear purchase/subscription terms; accessibility and error/recovery testing; accurate icon/screenshots/listing/content rating; production signing and artifact hash; physical-device smoke test where applicable; real support/privacy contacts; and required owner developer-account declarations.

Sensitive data must be minimized and never exposed in source or logs. Connected access must be revocable. Consequential/destructive actions require appropriate confirmation. The product must not claim guaranteed outcomes or professional medical, legal, financial, emergency, or security advice unless actually qualified and approved for that use.

This control is not proof by itself. Any failed or unverified applicable item remains a release blocker. Final approval is the store's decision.
