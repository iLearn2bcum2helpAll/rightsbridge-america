# Store Release Compliance

Last updated: 2026-10-07

Release is blocked until every applicable production requirement is verified: build/test/lint, current platform requirements, least-privilege permissions, accurate privacy/data-safety declarations, deletion/revocation where applicable, secure secrets/network/backups/logging, truthful claims, transparent commerce, accessibility/error/recovery testing, accurate store assets/content rating, signing/artifact hash/device smoke test, real support/privacy contacts, and owner store-console declarations.

Sensitive data must be minimized and never exposed in source or logs. Consequential actions require appropriate confirmation. This control is not proof by itself; any failed or unverified applicable item remains a blocker, and final approval is the store's decision.
